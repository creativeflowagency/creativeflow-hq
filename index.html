Plan

The business plan from September 30, 2026. Ask Claude to update it as things change.

Positioning

CreativeFlow owns pre-production: idea → hook → title & thumbnail → script → schedule.

"Where your next video gets planned before you press record." For creators with roughly 0–100k followers, posting weekly, without a team.

Pricing to test

  • Starter ~$14.99/mo: no or limited Adonis
  • Pro ~$29.99/mo: full Adonis, planner, Customize
  • Founding member ~$19.99/mo locked for life, first 50, with the OG badge
  • Annual billing with 2 months free; 7-day free trial

Getting members

  • Build in public: devlogs and shorts about making the app
  • Plan your own videos in CreativeFlow on camera
  • Affiliates: ~30% recurring for referrals (Gumroad supports it)
  • Member Discord, leaderboard and badges
  • Beta tester feedback → testimonials (with permission)

Numbers to watch

  • Members and monthly revenue
  • Churn under ~8% a month
  • Activation: plans a first video within 24 hours
  • Adonis cost per member (budget ~$10)

Unit economics at 25 members ($49.99)

  • Revenue ≈ $1,250 / month
  • Gumroad fees ≈ $140–165 · Adonis ≈ $150–300 · Netlify ≈ $20–50 · Domain ≈ $3
  • Profit ≈ $750–930 / month before income tax and your Claude plan
  • Worst case (everyone maxes Adonis at 35/day): still ≈ $300+

Next 90 days

  • Month 1: Netlify + sign-in fixed, beta test, decide pricing
  • Month 2: founding-member spots, post the VSL, devlogs, affiliates
  • Month 3: 25 paying members, ship the leaderboard, collect testimonials
